## Goal

## What changed
Committed by KINKIN-007. The Oct 12 work was committed by Thor1006, so two people now commit here.
- `a77b202` (Oct 13) **Multilingual UI, help guide, language flags.**
  - The UI comes in English, ไทย and 繁體中文（香港）. The text lives in `src/i18n/catalog.ts` (467 pairs) and is translated by `I18n.tsx` (`ui(...)` wraps JSX), using `messages.ts`.
  - The language choice is saved as `trf-language`.
  - `LanguageSelector.tsx` has SVG flags. The new `shell/HelpGuide.tsx` dialog has 4 topics and a larger-text toggle.
  - The check is `scripts/check-i18n.cjs`.
- `fbd60c6` (Oct 14) **Screen and popup transitions.** Shared `data-motion` styles live in `styles/global.css` and `theme.css`, and keyed remounts in `App.tsx` and `Root.tsx` trigger them.
- `0c20c8d` (Oct 14) **Session summaries.**
  - `state/SessionReport.ts` keeps whole-session statistics separate from the live buffers: first, mean, last and change, plus a trend capped at 240 averaged points.
  - It counts reps from `rep_end` events and kept apart from the kit's own counter. It flags data as partial when the page joined late, messages were lost, or values were invalid.
  - `shell/SessionSummary.tsx` adds the summary dialog, a CSV export (with a BOM, every cell quoted, formulas made harmless) and printing to PDF.
  - The check is `scripts/check-session-report.cjs`.
- `793d057` (Oct 14) **Five saved sessions.** `state/SessionReportHistory.ts` keeps the latest 5 summaries in localStorage (`trf-session-history`, version 1). Saved data is treated as untrusted: broken records are skipped with a warning, and a blocked or full storage leaves the history working in memory.
- **Uncommitted (8 files): Delete a saved session.**
  - `SessionReportTracker.remove()` and `SensorStore.deleteSessionReport()` add the delete itself.
  - A `completed` set (up to 64 entries) stops a late message from bringing a deleted report back.
  - `SessionSummary.tsx` asks "Delete this saved session?", with Cancel focused first, Escape to cancel, and a status line after deleting.
  - The summary strip now shows only once the dashboard is connected.
  - New tests were added to `check-session-report.cjs`, and the README was updated.
