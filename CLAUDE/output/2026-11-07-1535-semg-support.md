# Report: sEMG (MyoWare) support across the kit, and Pi set v0.2

**Date:** 2026-11-07 15:35 · **Result:** ✅ done in software (⚠️ the MyoWare itself hasn't run it yet) · **Plan:** [plans/2026-11-07-semg-support.md](../plans/2026-11-07-semg-support.md)

## Goal
The user: "can we make it support sEMG? IMU have some problem right now". Make the MyoWare a working ground-truth
sensor from its firmware to the Pi set, so radar + sEMG sessions can train fatigue without the IMU or camera.
Mid-task: "I also plugged in the exSSD so put the Pi firmware there too". The only external drive connected was
the PortableSSD, so the Pi set v0.2 went there.

## What changed (9 commits, pushed in 5 chunks)
1. **MyoWare firmware** (`firmware/myoware_record`, 24402a1):
   - Wi-Fi link like the IMU's fw 2.1: `truerehab-myoware.local`, TCP port 3334, one client.
   - Sampling on core 1 never waits; a core-0 task sends whole lines from a 64 KB buffer every ~20 ms.
   - `# status` line every 5 s (rate, skipped, Wi-Fi, RSSI, dropped lines).
   - Bluetooth stays with `WIFI_LINK 0`. Added `wifi_secrets.example.h`.
2. **Pi bridge** (d925126, b9788ff):
   - **One recording for all sensors:** the bridge owns the session folder (`recording.py`) and providers add their
     files. `record_dir` moves to `[bridge]`; v0.1's `[providers.radar] record_dir` is still read.
   - **`emg` provider:**
     - the envelope (50/s);
     - the median frequency (4/s), with the model's own `window_mdf`, only during movement;
     - health in plain words;
     - `emg.csv` written exactly like the recorder's `--emg`.
   - **`fake_myoware.py`:** talks like the board, with FAKE sEMG from `trnet.synth`.
3. **Model** (1f502e6):
   - Reps come from the sEMG envelope when there's no pose or IMU, or when those find no reps.
   - The check says "from the sEMG envelope (fallback)" and "can train: fatigue ✓".
   - The export leaves `romDeg` out instead of writing NaN, which the browser can't read.
4. **Dashboard** (6562461):
   - English, Thai and Traditional Chinese for the sEMG names and the MyoWare's messages.
   - Translator fix: a pattern no longer swallows a whole " · " message.
   - New manifest flag `intermittent`: the median frequency shows "Waiting for movement" at rest, not a red
     "Stopped".
5. **Pi set v0.2** (421808b, f6e0d12, 42f4fe8):
   - **Config:** `[providers.emg]` (off until the board runs the sketch).
   - **Installer:** points out a missing `[providers.emg]` in an older `config.toml`.
   - **Self-test:** checks the MyoWare, and has a `--config` option.
   - **README** updated, and the bundle now includes the MyoWare sketch.
   - **Bug found while verifying:** a radar *replay* that looped during a recording made the frame numbers jump
     back (check_session: −183 fps, 1667 missing). Fixed: each loop keeps counting.

## Issues / not done
- **The MyoWare has never run this sketch.** Uploading needs three things:
  - the board on USB with **nobody wearing it**;
  - `wifi_secrets.h` next to the sketch;
  - your "upload it".
- **Untested on a real signal:** the sEMG levels and thresholds (activity gate 9×; rep rule 15× noise and 30 % swing)
  come from fake data. Check them after the first real recording.
- **sEMG reps are a fallback.** Curls without a pause can merge into one; a dip at the top of a curl can split one.
  `check_session` compares the count with the space markers.
- **Still not tested (as before):** live radar, a real Pi.
- **"48 of 45" radar frames/s** in replay: the same small overshoot as in v0.1 (bursty replay timing).
- **On the SSD, for you to delete if you like:**
  - old bundles: three v0.1 (`611b50f`, `8ff8c11-modified`, `de84695`) and two v0.2 intermediates (`421808b`,
    `f6e0d12`);
  - test recordings in `test-install/recordings/`.
  - Final: `truerehab-pi-0.2.0-20261107-42f4fe8.tar.gz`.
  - Test-only config: `test-install/truerehab-pi/pi-bridge/config.semg-test.toml`.
